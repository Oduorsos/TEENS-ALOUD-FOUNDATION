Who We Are

Raising a generation of bold, purpose-driven, and spiritually grounded young people.

Transforming Lives Through Faith, Purpose & Love

Teens Aloud Foundation Kenya is a non-denominational and inter-denominational Christian youth foundation focused on empowering teenagers and young people to discover purpose, grow spiritually, and positively influence society.

Through mentorship programs, evangelism, discipleship, outreach missions, leadership training, and community service, we seek to nurture a generation that lives with love, faith, purpose, excellence, confidence, integrity, compassion, loyalty and vision.

Our Programs

Vision & Mission

Raising a generation grounded in Christ, purpose, leadership, and positive impact.

Our Vision

To challenge a young generation to believe in their gifted purpose and passionately pursue Jesus Christ to take His life-giving message to the ends of the earth.

Our Mission

To accomplish our vision, we set out to worship God intimately whilst using our talents and energies as young people to promote the gospel globally primarily to other young people through relevant modern ministries and events.

Our Programs

We impact lives through youth-centered programs designed to nurture growth, leadership, and spiritual transformation.

Tehila Kabash!

Tehila is a vibrant worship and creative arts event organized by Aloud Choir and Aloud Creative to provide young people with a platform to showcase their God-given talents and gifts. Through singing, dancing, spoken word, rap, drawing, drama, and other creative expressions, the event inspires young people to use their abilities to glorify God, build confidence, encourage others, and positively impact their generation in a fun, energetic, and spiritually uplifting atmosphere.

Learn more

New Breed X

New Breed is a special thanksgiving event organized for Form Four finalists and graduates as they transition into a new season of life. The event creates an atmosphere of gratitude, reflection, worship, and encouragement, reminding young people that finishing form four is not the end, but the beginning of purpose, responsibility, and greater impact.
Through mentorship, inspirational sessions, worship, testimonies, and fellowship, New Breed empowers graduates to boldly step into their future with faith, vision, excellence, and a strong relationship with God while preparing them for the next phase of their academic, personal, and spiritual journey.

Learn more

Mega Love Fellowship

Mega Love Fellowship is a powerful gathering that brings together members from different Love Fellowships for a time of fellowship, worship, mentorship, and spiritual empowerment. The event creates an opportunity for young people from various communities, schools, campuses, and fellowships to connect, grow together, and strengthen their faith in Christ.
Through impactful teachings, interactive sessions, worship experiences, games, and networking, Mega Love Fellowship inspires unity, love, accountability, and purpose among young people while building a stronger generation passionate about God and positive influence.

Learn more

TWC (Teenbuilders' Worship Camp)

TeenBuilders Worship Camp (TWC) is a transformative camp experience organized for university students to help them grow spiritually, refresh mentally, nurture their talents, and reconnect with purpose away from the pressures of campus life. Through worship, mentorship, fellowship, talent development, and impactful sessions, TWC creates a refreshing environment where young people are strengthened, inspired, and empowered for both life and faith.

Learn more

FWC (Freshers' Worship Camp)

Freshers Worship Camp (FWC) is a welcoming and life-impacting camp organized for students who are new to university and campus life. The camp helps freshers transition smoothly into this new phase by strengthening them spiritually, building meaningful connections, guiding them through campus challenges, and encouraging them to stay grounded in faith, purpose, and positive values while discovering their talents and potential.

Learn more

Pretesh Adventure Camp

Pretesh Adventure Camp is a fun, engaging, and spiritually enriching camp organized for high school students during school holidays and half-term breaks. The camp provides a safe and inspiring environment where teenagers can grow in their relationship with God, build friendships, discover their talents, receive mentorship, and enjoy refreshing activities that positively shape their character, purpose, and future.

Learn more

Jam The Streets

Jam the Street is a compassion and outreach event focused on showing love and support to street families and vulnerable communities through acts of kindness and generosity. Through food donations, financial support, and community contributions, young people come together to share meals, essentials, hope, and encouragement with those living on the streets while demonstrating the love of Christ through practical impact and service.

Learn more

Local Missions

Local Mission is an outreach initiative where young people raise funds and travel to different towns, cities and teen prone areas across Kenya to share the Gospel of Jesus Christ. Through street marches (Jesus Match), dance, music, talents, open-air evangelism, mentorship, and community interaction, the mission creates opportunities to impact lives, spread hope, and demonstrate the love of Christ while empowering young people to use their gifts for ministry and transformation.

Learn more

Jesus' Shopping

Jesus' Shopping is a charity outreach initiative focused on sharing love and care with street families and vulnerable communities through the donation of clothes, shoes, and basic utilities such as soap, tissue, and other essential items. Through the support of young people and well-wishers, the program seeks to restore dignity, spread hope, and demonstrate the love of Christ through practical acts of compassion and generosity.

Learn more

Hangouts

Each sub ministry or fellowship regularly organizes hangouts and fellowship gatherings designed to mentor, encourage, and build strong relationships among members while creating a fun and refreshing environment for young people. Through interactive activities, bonding sessions, worship, mentorship, games, and meaningful conversations, these hangouts help members grow spiritually, emotionally, socially, and personally as a family united in Christ.

Learn more

Executive Retreats

Executive Retreats are special leadership gatherings organized to refresh, strengthen, and realign leaders with the vision and mission of Teens Aloud Foundation. The retreats provide a space for reflection, strategic discussions, mentorship, spiritual growth, team bonding, and communication on organizational progress, upcoming plans, and new developments while equipping leaders to serve with renewed passion, unity, and purpose.

Learn more

Associates Fellowship

Associates Fellowship is a fellowship gathering designed for fourth-year students, finalists, graduates, and those transitioning from university life into the next phase of their journey. The fellowship provides a supportive space for mentorship, spiritual growth, networking, encouragement, and meaningful conversations as members prepare for life after campus while remaining grounded in faith, purpose, and positive impact.

Our Core Values

The principles that guide our mission, leadership, and impact among young people.

FAITH

We dare to trust God’s word as having unseen creative power to deliver tangible results. (Hebrews 11:1).

LOVE

We see each other as Christ saw us to give His life to us. (1 Jn 4:20-21).

INTEGRITY

We are the righteousness of God so our lives in secret reflect what we profess. (Ephesians 5:8, Proverbs 11:3).

VISION

We are quick to forget the yesterday in order to focus on the new and bigger things God is always doing. (Philippians 3:13).

PURPOSE

We believe that before we were born, God created us in Christ Jesus to fulfill a sure assignment on earth. (Ephesians 2:10).

LOYALTY

We believe that Faithfulness, Availability and Teachability are key requirements for discipleship. (Ruth 1:16).

TEENSPIRATION

We believe that God has a special plan for Teenagers and young people for that matter in these times, so we focus on reaching them especially. (Matthew 19:14).

EXCELLENCE

We demand of ourselves uncompromising quality and strive to be people who demonstrate the God standard in everything we do (Colossians 3:23).
